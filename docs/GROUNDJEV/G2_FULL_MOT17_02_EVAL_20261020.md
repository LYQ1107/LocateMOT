## Independence boundary

`MOT17-02` is a TRAIN-scope video for the Clean data contract. The overlap audit
found exact TRAIN frame/path overlap for frames 302–305 and 602 TRAIN rows from
the same video. The expanded 600-frame result is consequently a full
TRAIN-scope development diagnostic, not an independent generalization result.
The short 4-frame controls remain diagnostic only. A video-disjoint development
video must be evaluated before making a held-out or paper-level claim.
